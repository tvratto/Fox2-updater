FOX2 Firmware Updater App only

This updater writes ONLY the app at 0x10000. It does not touch bootloader, partitions, SPIFFS, or Preferences.

  1. Unplug the device.
  2. Hold BOOT, then plug USB in. Keep holding BOOT for ~2 seconds, then release.
  3. Click Connect and choose the USB device/port.
  4. Select firmware, then click Flash App Update.
  5. If the device doesn’t restart automatically, press RESET once.
Important: This page will never erase your device. If you used a generic flasher before and “nothing happened” on boot, it likely wrote to the wrong address or erased flash (which can wipe Preferences).
