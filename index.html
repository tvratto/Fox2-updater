FOX2 Firmware Updater (App Only)

This updater writes ONLY the app at 0x10000. It does not erase bootloader, partitions, SPIFFS, or Preferences.

  1. Unplug the device.
  2. Hold BOOT while plugging in USB.
  3. Click Connect.
  4. Select firmware and click Flash.
  5. Press RESET if needed.
This tool will NEVER erase your device.
