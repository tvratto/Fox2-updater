FOX2 Firmware Updater App-only @ 0x10000

This flashes only the application at 0x10000 (like your working esptool script). It does not erase all, and should preserve partitions/SPIFFS/NVS.

  1. Unplug the device.
  2. Hold BOOT, then plug USB in. Keep holding for ~2 seconds, then release.
  3. Click Connect and pick the USB device.
  4. Select firmware and click Flash.
  5. If it doesn’t restart, press RESET once.
